FlipDash Support

Tap to flip gravity. Dodge the spikes. Beat your best.

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Send us a message and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to flip gravity between the floor and the ceiling. Land on the side without spikes. Tap again while in the air to reverse the flip once.

I bought "Remove Ads" but still see ads

Open the game, go to Settings (gear icon) or the Shop and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. The optional reward videos ("Continue" and "Double coins") stay available on purpose. They only play when you tap them.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. On any iPhone you can also go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The leaderboard doesn't show my score

Leaderboards use Game Center. Make sure you are signed in under iOS Settings → Game Center. Scores are sent when a run ends; the weekly board resets every Monday.

Will I lose my progress?

Coins, skins and your best score are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
